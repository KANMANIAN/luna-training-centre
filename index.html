About our centre

Education, Skills & Services for Everyone

LUNA Training Centre is an academic and vocational learning centre established in 2024. We aim to empower our local community through quality education, skill development, and essential digital services.

Our goal is to create a welcoming place where learners can strengthen their knowledge, practise new skills, and take confident steps toward their goals.

  • Student-focused academic support
  • Practical computer and typing training
  • Reading and writing improvement practice
  • Convenient document and digital services
