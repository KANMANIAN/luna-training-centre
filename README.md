# LUNA TRAINING CENTRE

Learn • Develop • Achieve

Established in 2024, Luna Training Centre, Kadayanallur, provides quality education, computer training, vocational courses, and essential digital services. We offer tuition classes for students from 1st to 12th standard, computer courses from basic to advanced levels, spoken English training, and practical skill development programs.

Our centre also provides Xerox, printing, and e-Sevai services to support the needs of the local community. With individual attention, practical learning, and a student-friendly environment, we aim to empower learners with the knowledge and skills they need for a brighter future.
